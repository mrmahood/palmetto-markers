# Contributing

The atlas is meant to stay readable without an account. Contributions are **sourced historical assets** — photographs, maps, documents — that belong to the story on the marker, not snapshots of the plaque itself.

## What we want

1. A public-domain or clearly licensed image of the *subject* (the mill, the person, the church, the battle)
2. A caption: who/what, when, where
3. A durable source (Library of Congress item URL, NARA, HABS survey, museum catalog)
4. Honesty when the plate is related but not the exact subject

## What we do not want

- AI-generated historical scenes or invented portraits
- Uncredited web scrapes
- Replacing the official inscription
- Votes as a substitute for review

## Code

Issues and pull requests against this repo are welcome. Keep changes small. Do not commit `.env`, API keys, or `node_modules`.

On a lesson, sign in and use **Source a photograph**. Submissions stay pending until an editor accepts them. You can also open an issue with the marker id (e.g. `35-11`), the image URL, and the credit line.
